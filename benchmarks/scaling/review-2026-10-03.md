budget: **64D sampling remains a throughput bottleneck**. Do not advertise the
65% figure as applying to this sampler; lag1 overlap does not erase serialized
sampling cost.

## Million-trial volume and billing

The selected sampler and final stop-path binary complete 1,000,000 trials at
64 continuous dimensions, W=8, batch8/lag1, and a 3-ms synthetic objective in
768.90 s. Peak RSS is 21,292 KiB (20.79 MiB). Proposal time is 690.708 s and
direct barrier time 58.915 s. All 1,000,000 received billing lines strict-parse
as complete, unique, monotonic terminal records; the stream is 2,145,834,929
bytes with SHA-256
`52c216e7bac8a3ebdce349488f0cfd79eeffc84cdfe6c7ffa1ce363dae17d92d`.
Serialization costs 55.992 us/line and writing 4.094 us/line. Final JSON is
22,887 bytes, rendering in 0.492 ms and writing in 0.108 ms. The volume and
bounded-memory gate passes; the long-budget quality gate does not.

## Coverage and stop-path fixes

Exact finite coverage uses a lazy dense bitset through cardinality 100,000,000,
at most 12.5 MB/index, instead of disk insertion inside the run-state mutex.
Larger/unknown spaces retain the disk fallback. Boundary/reset/duplicate and
out-of-range behavior are tested. In a 100k-trial four-input finite native run,
the pre-review binary takes 3.18 s and the bitset binary 3.07 s; billing bytes
are identical (42,756,665). The speedup on this machine is modest, not the
review's loaded 137-us/trial extrapolation. Dense coverage raises that run's
RSS from 4,676 to 16,636 KiB by allocating the 100-million-bit index.

The one-million-insert microprobe measures 0.014506 us/insert for the dense
index versus 3.01059 us/insert for the disk fallback: 0.0145 s versus 3.0106 s
total. These direct insertion measurements do not include run-state contention.

Timeout insertion into the archive and enqueue into the terminal stream now
share one atomic critical section after queue capacity is available. Progress
readiness polls are bounded at 50 ms; stopped/stalled readers receive a two-second
grace, then an explicit I/O failure. The coordinator wakes every 50 ms while
waiting on workers so SIGTERM is observed even under a race detector. Pipes
require each serialized record to fit `PIPE_BUF`, rejecting larger records
before writing any fragment; regular output files have no such restriction.
Billing uses delivered complete lines, never final-result counts after I/O failure.
The cached trials-file flag is immutable. Quality ctest timeout is 300 seconds;
0.3.0 is correctly marked released.

## Reproduction and evidence

`quality_long.py` runs/compares the pinned replica, and rejects stale cached
studies if a binary changes. `full_history_reference.py` materializes the
benchmark-only unbounded-retention copy and records the exact replacement and
source hashes. Use the same frozen coordinator extraction for both quality
builds. `profile.cpp`, `ordinal_profile.cpp`, and `run_native.py --finite` cover
the acquisition, insertion, and native finite probes. Native before/after
finite probes both use bounded output and drain the billing stream.

Compact per-seed quality and timing evidence with verified SHA-256 sidecars is
published under `../optuna/evidence/2026-10-03-review-*.csv`. Raw study traces,
full test logs, and smoke metadata remain review artifacts, not public paths.
